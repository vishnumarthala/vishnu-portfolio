## Platform

web

## Stack

Next.js (App Router, TypeScript), vanilla CSS / CSS Modules, deployed on Vercel. Bilingual EN + DE with a language switch.

## Users

Primary: enterprise AI peers, recruiters, and conference/speaking contacts who want to know who Vishnu Marthala is, how he thinks about AI architecture, and how to reach him. Secondary: German employers and HR looking to hire skilled people or Ausbildung candidates from India through IndoFachkraft.

## Product Purpose

The personal site of Vishnu Marthala, AI Solution Architect at Roche in Ludwigsburg and founder of IndoFachkraft. It presents two connected tracks (enterprise AI architecture, and a talent bridge from India to Germany) as one way of thinking: clear architecture. Success is a visitor getting in touch by email or LinkedIn.

## Positioning

A certified software architect (iSAQB CPSA) who has worked in Germany since 2014, combines enterprise AI and GenAI architecture at Roche with running his own India to Germany recruitment ventures, and has built a Telugu-language RAG platform over a newspaper archive. Few people can truthfully claim both the enterprise AI and the India to Germany talent bridge.

## Operating Context

Visitors arrive from LinkedIn, conference contexts, email signatures, and IndoFachkraft. They scan for role, credibility, and contact details. German employers want to send a concrete hiring request (company, areas, positions, start) without hunting for an address; everyone else wants WhatsApp, email or a call in one step. German visitors expect an Impressum and Datenschutz page and may prefer German.
